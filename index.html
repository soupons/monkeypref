Pull a judge pool from Tabroom

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: monkeypref (or right-click → bookmark link)
  2. Log in to Tabroom and open a tournament's Judges list for your event, e.g. tabroom.com/index/tourn/judges.mhtml?category_id=…
  3. Click the bookmark. It reads every judge and paradigm using your logged-in session, then opens them here. A pool with ~50 judges takes about 10 seconds.

Everything stays in this browser. Re-running the bookmark on the same pool refreshes paradigms and keeps your checkmarks. You can also someone shared.

This page is open as a local file, and browsers won't let Tabroom open a local file. Host it first (e.g. drop the folder on Netlify), then grab the bookmark from the hosted page.
